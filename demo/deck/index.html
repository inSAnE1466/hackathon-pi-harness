The problem

An EU health and travel insurer wants AI on its claims queue. It has two bad options.

Frontier models only

Best reasoning

Sending health data to them unchecked breaks GDPR Art. 9 and the insurers' code of conduct.

Local models only

Data stays inside

The insurer gives up the quality of the best models on every claim, including the harmless ones.

Business case

Handlers stop reading every page. They approve a prepared, cited recommendation.

What the agent prepares

Completeness, extraction of billable items, coverage against policy terms, with the clause cited.

What stays with people

Medical necessity goes to the medical adviser. Every pay, reduce, deny or document request goes to the claims handler.

Cost of the check
587 ms

median routing decision per request on the golden set.

The policy is data (policy/brane.json), so the legal team changes a rule without a code change, and every change is measured on the golden set before it is kept.
